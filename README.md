### Hi, I'm Chris Holland

Air Force veteran · Data Science & Machine Learning student (University of Phoenix, 2027) · Founder of [Credit Comeback Club](https://creditcomebackclub.com)

I spent years in the credit industry before I studied data science, and it changed how I see a credit report. It isn't a verdict. It's the serving layer at the end of a pipeline: court records scraped by vendors, packaged, and sold to bureaus, with almost no validation or reconciliation back to the source. Most of what I build lives in that gap between what the data says and what's actually true.

---

#### What I'm building

**[AURA](https://github.com/creditcomebackclub/aura-ai)**: a proactive AI assistant with hybrid memory retrieval (vector + lexical + entity + recency + confidence), inspectable retrieval traces, and evidence-gated learning. New behaviors have to pass replayed evaluations before they go live, and a belief needs at least two independent episodes before it's trusted.

**[CCC Forensic Suite](https://github.com/creditcomebackclub/ccc-forensic-demo)**: a full-stack operations platform for credit repair. It runs AI-assisted forensic audits of credit reports against Metro 2 field references, a four-phase dispute and escalation pipeline, and client-facing progress tracking.

**CCC Dispute Outcomes Analysis** *(in progress)*: a statistical study of what actually gets an inaccurate tradeline corrected, using logistic regression and survival analysis on de-identified dispute outcomes.

#### What I care about
